## Required evidence and semantics

Confirmed positive means the current limited numerical gate is supported by a complete dated single homogeneous material assay and strictly above BOTH class criteria. It is only a provisional Class 7 characteristic, not transport clearance. Negative means the complete assessed material does not meet one/both strict conditions; it NEVER establishes the consignment is not dangerous goods. No measured sample, no isotope identification, unknown progeny, insufficient material mass, unknown source date, no other fissile mass inventory or out-of-scope geometry produces missing/unsupported evidence, not a negative. A claimed approval, special form, low dispersible status, natural-ore exemption, fissile exception or special arrangement is not treated as approved. It requires complete certificates and competent-authority/expert confirmation.

## Source-of-truth boundary

The UN Manual of Tests and Criteria Revision 8 (ST/SG/AC.10/11/Rev.8) and Amendment 1 (ST/SG/AC.10/11/Rev.8/Amend.1) supplied to the team do not replace ADR 2025 radioactive classification limits or package approval clauses. No GHS/CLP automatic conversion is allowed.

**Critical unresolved point:** SCO concerns a solid object that is NOT itself radioactive (2.2.7.1.3). Its surface survey cannot safely be treated as an ordinary homogeneous-material Bq/g gate derived from the object's full mass. The separate SCO screen remains a candidate and needs a specialized approved input path before general use.
